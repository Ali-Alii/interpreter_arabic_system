A note on pronunciation and accuracy

Speak the ending you want transcribed, even when it is grammatically unusual. The system uses an audio-trained model, but it cannot guarantee verbatim endings. Compare the output with your recording. Long recordings use overlapping chunks when needed. If a join is uncertain, review the indicated audio range; repeated words or conflicting endings may remain. Fatḥatan before a supporting alif is displayed on the final alif, as requested. The original model output remains available above.

To establish accuracy, use the included evaluation script with recordings and human-checked references, including incorrect endings and paused speech. General diacritic benchmarks do not establish final-word accuracy for this app.
